Le Bel Canto

A showcase website for a family pizzeria in Saint-Lô. The 110-dish menu reads in photos, and a search by ingredient helps guests choose in seconds.

  • ContextConcept demo, made without a commission
  • PeriodOctober 2026
  • My roleUX/UI, front-end build, translation, local SEO
  • ToolsHTML, CSS, JavaScript, Lighthouse, Search Console

View the live demo ↗

In 30 seconds · Concept demo

  1. The need

    Show a long menu without losing the visitor, and get tables booked.

  2. What I did

    110 dishes in photos, search by ingredient, FR/EN, light and dark, map on click.

  3. The result

    Live demo, Lighthouse in the green on mobile and desktop.

Key numbers

  • 110dishes in photos
  • 8menu sections
  • FR+ENtwo languages
  • 2themes, light and dark

The context

A family pizzeria on rue du Belle, well known locally but hard to find online.

The challenge

Make people hungry, show a long menu without losing the visitor, and get tables booked by phone.

My role

  • Interface and mood design: dark background, the house gold, a thin Italian line.
  • HTML, CSS and JavaScript build, with a search by ingredient.
  • Full English translation, dish names included.
  • Local SEO, Google Search Console, Bing and Lighthouse quality checks.

01 / 06

Mood first

The dining room is warm and softly lit. I kept that mood: a dark background, the house gold and a thin green, white and red line.

Context A warm dining room, hard to find online.

02 / 06

A long menu, made simple

110 items is a lot on a phone. I added a search by ingredient that keeps only the dishes that contain it.

The menu in one word Type an ingredient: only the matching dishes stay.

03 / 06

Lunch deals

Menu Express, Menu Bambino and the pizza of the month are highlighted: they are what bring guests in at lunchtime.

Lunch deals Menu Express, Menu Bambino and pizza of the month.

04 / 06

Made for the phone

An action bar that is always visible: book, see the menu, get there. Today’s hours show straight away.

Made for the phone Book, see the menu and find the street with your thumb.

05 / 06

Two languages, two lights

Dark by default, light on demand and remembered. The English version translates the dish names too.

Dark at night, light by day Two themes and a menu fully in English.

06 / 06

Quality and launch

Fonts hosted on the server, Google Maps loaded only on click, bilingual sitemap, Search Console and Bing. A “Site by CALYNEVO” credit stays in the footer.

Delivery Real Lighthouse scores and the list of what was delivered.

2026

October update

The 23 dishes without a photo now have one (illustration photos). Directions start from a photo card of the shop front, which opens Google Maps only on click.

A concept demo, not a commission

I made this site without a commission, to show my skills to the business. It will be removed on simple request.
